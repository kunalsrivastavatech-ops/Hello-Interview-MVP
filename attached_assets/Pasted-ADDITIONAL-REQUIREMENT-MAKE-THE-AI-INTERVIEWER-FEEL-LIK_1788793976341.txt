ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENT — MAKE THE AI INTERVIEWER FEEL LIKE A REAL HUMAN

The female AI interviewer must not feel like a static photograph or a simple talking avatar.

I want her to behave like a REALISTIC FICTIONAL HUMAN INTERVIEWER who is conducting the interview naturally.

While she is speaking:
- Her lips must synchronize with the actual voice.
- She should naturally blink her eyes.
- Her facial expressions should subtly change according to her speech.
- She should make small, natural head movements.
- She should have realistic micro-expressions.
- Where appropriate, she should make subtle hand gestures while asking or explaining a question.
- Her shoulders/body should have very subtle natural movement.
- Avoid exaggerated animation.
- Movements should be professional and realistic, like an actual corporate interviewer.

Example:

When asking:
"Could you walk us through a technical project you are proud of?"

She should:
- Look toward the candidate.
- Blink naturally.
- Move her lips according to the actual words.
- Make a subtle hand gesture while asking the question.
- Have natural facial movement.
- Slightly move her head/body.
- Stop speaking naturally when the audio ends.

When listening:
- Stop mouth movement.
- Maintain eye contact toward the candidate.
- Blink naturally.
- Have subtle breathing/head movement.
- Look attentive and engaged.
- Do NOT continue speaking animation.

When thinking/evaluating:
- Slight natural change in expression.
- Small head movement.
- Natural blinking.
- No exaggerated cartoon-like animation.

IMPORTANT:
The goal is NOT to make a cartoon character.

The goal is:

"A realistic fictional AI-generated female interviewer who behaves like a real human interviewer."

She should look and behave naturally enough that the candidate feels like they are actually sitting across from an interviewer.

Prioritize realism:
1. Accurate lip synchronization with speech
2. Natural eye blinking
3. Natural facial expressions
4. Subtle head movement
5. Natural hand gestures
6. Subtle body/shoulder movement
7. Professional interviewer behavior

Avoid:
- robotic movement
- repetitive blinking
- constant mouth opening/closing
- exaggerated hand movements
- cartoon animation
- emoji-style animation
- unnatural head shaking
- looping movements that obviously repeat
