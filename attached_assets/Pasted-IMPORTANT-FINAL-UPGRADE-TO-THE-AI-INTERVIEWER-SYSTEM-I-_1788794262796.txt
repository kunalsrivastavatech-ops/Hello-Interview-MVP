==================================================

This is the highest priority visual requirement.

The lips must correspond to the actual spoken audio.

AI generates question
        ↓
TTS generates audio
        ↓
AI interviewer SPEAKING state
        ↓
Actual audio starts
        ↓
Lips/mouth animate according to speech
        ↓
Audio ends
        ↓
Mouth stops
        ↓
AI interviewer becomes LISTENING

Use the actual audio playback lifecycle.
